The Layout

DESIGNED AROUND HOW YOU ACTUALLY LIVE.

Great architecture is measured in daily life. Across 9,292 square feet, every room at 9450 Bay Dr was planned with intention — a layout that is efficient, functional, and effortlessly livable, where nothing is wasted and everything is exactly where it should be.

Intelligent Flow

An intuitive progression from entry to water — the foyer opens to the great room, dining, and terrace in one continuous line, so the home reads instantly and moves naturally, without a single wasted corridor.

Family Functionality

Everyday spaces are placed where families actually use them — a chef's kitchen, breakfast room, and family areas are grouped at the heart of the home. Dedicated staff quarters and mudroom-style service areas keep daily life organized and out of sight.

Entertaining Spaces

Formal and casual areas connect without colliding. The great room, dining room, fully equipped outdoor kitchen, and cabana work alongside the 34-foot pool terrace as one continuous entertaining stage — for twelve guests or eighty.

Bedroom Separation

Eight bedrooms, thoughtfully arranged. The west-facing, bay-view primary suite is a true retreat, set apart from the private guest suite, dedicated home office, and family wings so privacy is built into the plan — not an afterthought.

Curated Arrival

A seamless transition from the motor court to an air-conditioned garage ensures your collection is preserved in perfect comfort, stepping effortlessly into the home's refined interior.

Indoor-Outdoor Connection

Floor-to-ceiling glass dissolves the line between interior and waterfront. Principal rooms open directly to the terrace, pool, and 75 feet of bay frontage — sunlight deep in the plan, water views from the spaces that matter.

The result is a home that performs as beautifully as it presents — planned once, planned right, and built for the way life actually happens.
